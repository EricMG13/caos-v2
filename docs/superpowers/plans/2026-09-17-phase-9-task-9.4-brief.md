## Contract and scope

1. **Pin validation** (`run_inputs._research`): a brief must carry every
   required key of `CP_DR_RESEARCH_BRIEF_V1`, `mode: linked`, `source_mode`
   equal to the supplied-only value, `scope_key` equal to the pinned
   subject's `issuer_id`, and every `consumer_module_id`/`after_module_id`
   among the pinned route's modules; otherwise `RUN_INPUT_INVALID` at pin.
   `run_id` and `cp0_sha256` are host-filled at prompt time, never trusted
   from the caller: the pin stores them absent and the executor fills
   `run_id` from `cos_run_id` and `cp0_sha256` from the accepted CP-0
   record's Markdown digest.
2. **Prompt** (`invocation.py`): for `module_id == "CP-DR"` only, a tagged
   host-owned section `RESEARCH BRIEF` carrying the filled brief JSON, after
   the upstream sections and inside the request ceiling; any other module's
   prompt is byte-identical to today's (pinned by test). `HostIdentity` gains
   the research front-matter fields the vendor's `prepare` emits
   (`research_mode`, `research_question`, `scope_type`, `scope_key`,
   `subject_name`, `source_mode`), filled from the brief; `invocation_fields`
   emits them for CP-DR.
3. **Contract**: a CP-DR fixture in `tests/canonical_route_fixtures.py`
   (TDR.1–TDR.3 from `load_contract`, quoting whole tokens of the pack); the
   five contract tests; CP-DR joins `ADAPTER_MODULES`.
4. **Route**: `tests/test_lite_deep_research_route.py` — completes, proves,
   freezes; a run pinned without a brief never reaches CP-DR
   (`EVIDENCE_NOT_AVAILABLE` before any attempt, no reservation); ceiling;
   the pair joins `ADAPTER_ROUTES`; the guard updated.
5. **Corpus and keys**: `qualification/vmo2-fy2025-deep-research/` with the
